# Marketing Lead Spec - AI Support Ops

## Go-To-Market
- Beta: 5 design-partner B2B SaaS support teams with 25-150 agents.
- Launch: move from private beta to controlled GA focused on support leaders and ops analysts.
- Growth: expand into regulated SaaS teams that need audit trails around AI use.

## Target Segments
| Segment | Size Signal | Pain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Series B-C SaaS support orgs | 30-150 agents | queue sprawl and slow onboarding |
| Enterprise SaaS support ops | 150-500 agents | auditability and approval controls |
| Platform support teams | high escalation volume | weak knowledge grounding |

## Messaging
- Primary value prop: faster support without hidden AI risk.
- Tagline: `Grounded AI assistance for support teams that need speed and accountability.`
- Proof themes: SLA protection, manager approvals, audit-ready AI telemetry, knowledge freshness.

## Launch Timeline
- Month 1: analyst interviews, design partner onboarding, proof dashboard.
- Month 2: beta stories, ROI calculator, compliance FAQ.
- Month 3: GA launch webinar, analyst brief, customer references.

## Channels
- LinkedIn and niche support ops communities.
- Partner referrals from CX consultants.
- SEO content around SLA, QA scorecards, and AI support governance.
- Webinar and roundtable with heads of support.

## Success Metrics
- 50 qualified demos in first quarter after launch.
- 30 percent demo-to-pilot conversion.
- 70 percent pilot activation within 21 days.
- CAC target under 4200 USD for mid-market segment.
